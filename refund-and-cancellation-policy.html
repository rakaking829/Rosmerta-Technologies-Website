Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: 13th April 2026

Refunds are applicable only in case where the damaged product or incorrect size of HSRP is processed. Returns and Replacements are done free of cost if wrong product, wrong size or damaged product are processed.

Return Intimation:

You can either call us at Support Number 8929722206 (Toll Free) or Email online@bookmyhsrp.com

The reason of return (providing Order number, and Reason) should be shared with the Customer Support by email.

Cancellation Policy:

Easy cancellation process is available for all our esteemed customers. You can cancel your online order within 30 minutes of ordering.

After successful cancellation charged amount for the order will be refunded within six-eight working days.

You can cancel an order in easy step just by clicking on order cancellation tab and submitting required details.

For any further query or clarity please call us on our Toll-Free No. 8929722206 or Email us at online@bookmyhsrp.com.

Please note that the complete order needs to be cancelled. We do not accept Partial order cancellation requests.

The Super Tag bought along with the HSRP cannot be cancelled stand alone as the same is bought as a package. If you decide to cancel the HSRP, the Tag order will also be deemed as cancelled. For Super Tag queries Contact support@MyParkplus.com

The Frames have been bought along with the HSRP, it cannot be cancelled stand alone as the same is bought as a package. If you decide to cancel the HSRP, the frames order will also be deemed as cancelled.

Refund Policy:

After successful cancellation, your order amount will be refunded within six- eight working days. Please note that the timing of actual account credit is dependent on multiple external agencies i.e. banks/payment gateways. The refund amount will be credited back into the same account that was used to make the payment.

If you have any questions about these Terms, please contact us at: contact@rosmertatech.com
