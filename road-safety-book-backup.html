Road Safety Children’s Book – FAQs

What is this Road Safety Children’s Book about?

This book is designed to teach children essential road safety rules through engaging real-life incidents, colourful illustrations, and relatable characters. It simplifies complex concepts like traffic signals, pedestrian rules, and basic road safety rules for children.

Who is the book suitable for?

The book is ideal for children aged 5–12 years, including primary and middle-school students. Teachers and parents can also use it as a learning aid for road safety lessons.

Why is road safety important for children?

Children are active, curious and often unaware of hazards. Teaching them road safety early helps prevent accidents and builds lifelong safe-movement habits.

How can this book be used in schools?

Schools can use it for classroom discussions, morning assemblies, library reading sessions, or during special events like Road Safety Week. It also works well as part of Life Skills or General Awareness curriculum.

Are there activities included for children?

Yes! The book includes worksheets, fun facts, quizzes and interactive activities that make learning engaging and practical.
