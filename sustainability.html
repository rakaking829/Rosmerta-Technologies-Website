Rosmerta Technologies' Rosblu is a premium solution designed for BS-VI and BS-IV upgraded diesel vehicles to meet stringent emission norms. Compliant with ISO-22241 and VDA standards, Rosblu ensures optimal performance and environmental compliance.

#3

Pollution Under Control (PUC)

Rosmerta Technologies offers compliant Pollution Under Control (PUC) services to ensure your vehicle meets environmental standards. Our advanced web application seamlessly syncs vehicle data with the Government of India's VAHAN portal, enabling real-time updates and accuracy.

Additionally, we ensure smooth integration of PUCC devices from various OEMs, guaranteeing comprehensive and reliable pollution control solutions.
