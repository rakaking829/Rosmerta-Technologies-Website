ACKO Drive

The ACKO Drive Awards recognise excellence across India’s automotive industry: from breakthrough vehicles and cutting-edge innovations to standout marketing campaigns. Central to this celebration is the TOTM (“The One That Matters”) designation, awarded to products, people and ideas that the jury deems truly standout. Through a rigorous, hands-on evaluation process led by industry experts, each winner is selected with transparency and real-world relevance in mind. Taken together, the Awards and TOTM construct a meaningful benchmark for trust, quality and informed decision-making within the automotive ecosystem.
