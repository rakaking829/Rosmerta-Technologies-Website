About 26th SAFE Annual Convention

Rosmerta Technologies is proud to participate in the 26th SAFE Annual Convention 2025 as a Platinum Partner, organized by the Society of Indian Automobile Manufacturers (SIAM) and its body, the Society for Automotive Fitness & Environment (SAFE - a SIAM initiative). The convention, scheduled for 18th September 2025 at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, from 9:30 AM to 12:30 PM, focuses on addressing road safety and environmental challenges through awareness campaigns, workshops, and collaborative programs with state governments.

Since its launch, SAFE has been a platform for road safety advocates, experts, and policymakers to share strategies that contribute to a safer road environment. The past editions in Odisha, Assam, and Karnataka have successfully driven awareness and encouraged action toward reducing accidents and improving compliance with safety norms.

We look forward to connecting with stakeholders at the 26th SAFE Annual Convention 2025 and sharing our solutions for advancing road safety, sustainability, and a more responsible mobility ecosystem.
