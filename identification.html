#3

Microdots

Rosmerta Technologies is proud to be the first company to launch Microdots in India, revolutionising vehicle security and part identification. Microdots can be applied on individual parts or the entire vehicle (Whole of Vehicle Marking - WOVM). Microdots offer theft protection, part identification, and traceability, making it easy to differentiate genuine parts from spurious ones. This innovative solution benefits the automotive and white goods industries, as well as insurance companies and banks, by enhancing security and authenticity verification.
