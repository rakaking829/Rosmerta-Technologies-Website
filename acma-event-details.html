About Event

Rosmerta Technologies is honoured to be a part of the 65th ACMA Annual Convention 2025 as a Support Partner. This partnership of ours with the Automotive Component Manufacturers Association of India (ACMA) reflects our adherence to innovation in digital mobility, vehicle compliance, and sustainability that align with ACMA’s mission of strengthening India’s automotive ecosystem.

At Rosmerta, we remain connected to leveraging innovation and technology to contribute to a safer, sustainable, and globally competitive future. The 65th ACMA Annual Convention hosted by the Automotive Component Manufacturers Association of India (ACMA) will be held on 12th September 2025 at Hotel Taj Palace, New Delhi.

The theme for this year's conference will be on Navigating Geopolitical Challenges: Creating a Resilient Automotive Supply Chain in India. A discussion that reflects the industry’s address to resolve global uncertainties while building resilience in India’s auto component sector. While featuring eminent ministers, industry veterans, insightful panels, and participation from over 1,500 delegates.
