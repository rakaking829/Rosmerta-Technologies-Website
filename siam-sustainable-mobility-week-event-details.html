SIAM Sustainable Mobility Week 2026

SIAM’s Conference on ‘Automotive Material Compliance & Sustainability’ is designed to serve as a pivotal platform for navigating the increasingly complex framework of global and domestic regulations, including IMDS protocols, REACH, and the management of chemical restrictions such as Persistent Organic Pollutants (POPs). The primary purpose of this conference is to foster cross-industry collaboration among OEMs, suppliers, and policymakers to establish robust mechanisms for material reporting and supply chain transparency. By participating, stakeholders will gain critical strategic insights into mitigating compliance risks, integrating bio-based and recycled materials, and fostering circular economy.
