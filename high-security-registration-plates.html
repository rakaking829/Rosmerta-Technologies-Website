Understanding the HSRP Layout & Features

Hot Stamped Chromium based Hologram

Hot-stamped, tamper proof, Chromium-based hologram on the top left corner of the plate

Ingressed IND Legend

International Registration Code of India

Laser Engraved Code

Unique identification marks (Laser code), 10-digit laser-etched PIN

Denotes the state where vehicle is registered

Denotes the district where vehicle is registered

Unique alpha-numeric combination code

Snap Locks

Affixation with non-removable, non-reusable Snap Locks

Embossed Alpha-Numerals

Alpha-numeric characters, are embossed followed by a hot-stamped film, bearing the inscription 'India' at a 45-degree angle

Reflective Sheeting

Enhanced visibility during day and night
