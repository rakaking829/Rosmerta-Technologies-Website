About Event

The Traffic Infra Tech Expo 2025 is set to return this October 2025. This shall bring together India’s most influential platform for traffic management, road construction, infrastructure, and parking solutions. Recognised as Asia’s largest event in this space, the Expo operates as a hub for knowledge, collaboration, and innovation. Bringing together government leaders, industry directors and global experts all under one roof.

As part of this exclusive event and gathering; We at Rosmerta Technologies will showcase our vision for smarter, safer, and greener mobility. Our expertise in digital mobility, vehicle compliance, and sustainable transport solutions positions us as a strong contributor to the Expo’s mission of advancing infrastructure and traffic technologies for India’s future.

Held alongside the Road Infratech Expo and Parking Infratech Expo, this year’s edition will also feature the Smart Mobility Conference, offering serious insights into global trends, road safety innovations, parking automation, and next-gen infrastructure planning. With over 80 exhibitors and wide B2B and B2G participation, the Traffic Infra Tech Expo Venue will act as the key platform to experience cutting-edge mobility solutions. For schedules, registrations, and updates, visit the official Traffic Infra Tech Expo 2025 website.
