About FADA 2025

The FADA event 2025 will be marking its 4th Finance & Insurance Summit and the 7th Auto Retail Conclave 2025. And Rosmerta Technologies is honoured to be the Title Partner for FADA 2025, reinforcing our shared commitment to innovation, collaboration and growth in the Indian automotive landscape.

The FADA conference is one of the top and most influential gatherings of the industry. This summit represents and speaks of the automobile industry globally, fostering dealer excellence and collaboration among OEMs, policymakers and finance and insurance stakeholders. Sharing concepts, advancements, experiences, latest technology updates, and deliberating on tactics to enhance India’s automotive ecosystem.

The 4th Finance & Insurance Summit and the 7th Auto Retail Conclave will take place on September 10, 2025, at Hotel Le Meridien in New Delhi. FADA Conference India aims to honour excellence in the industry and provide practical insights. The conclave will conclude with Dealer Satisfaction Study Survey Awards 2025 for OEMs, honouringcompanies that have the best dealer support, product quality and responsiveness to market changes.

Rosmerta Technologies, this collaboration with the Federation of Automobile Dealers' Associations of India at the FADA event 2025 marks our commitment towards the future technology of India, a consumer-focused and sustainable automotive future.
