Advanced Fire Detection & Suppression System for Buses

Reimagining Fire Safety. Redefining Passenger Protection.

RosRescu by Rosmerta Technologies is an advanced Fire Detection, Alarm & Suppression System (FDAS & FDSS) engineered specifically for buses to enhance passenger safety and reduce fire-related risks.

The intelligent system detects fire hazards at the earliest stage, instantly alerts the driver, and automatically activates suppression mechanisms before flames can escalate into major incidents.

Engineered to protect engine, battery and passenger compartments, RosRescu ensures safer journeys, lower risk, and regulatory compliance.

From early fire detection to rapid fire suppression, RosRescu ensures reliable protection when every second matters.
