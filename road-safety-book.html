Safe Roads Starts with You!

Children are among the most vulnerable road users in India, yet safety education often starts too late. Rosmerta believes that real road safety begins with awareness and habits formed early in life.

The Curious Bunch introduces essential road safety concepts in a simple, engaging way, helping children understand everyday situations and responsible behaviour on the road.

This initiative reflects Rosmerta’s commitment to nurturing a culture of safety and building a safer mobility future for India.
