Gyan Mandir Khelotsav 2025

We are thrilled to have partnered with Gyan Mandir Public School as the Road Safety Partner for Gyan Khelotsav 2025, the school’s annual sports festival.

Sports build discipline, teamwork, and resilience but none of it holds value without safety. At Rosmerta, we believe road safety and awareness are skills that should take root early, shaping confident and responsible citizens for the future.

During the festival, we connected with students, parents, and teachers through hands-on workshops, safety demonstrations, and interactive activities designed to build responsible road habits in a fun and engaging way.

The energy was infectious, the curiosity inspiring. Watching young learners lean into conversations about safety reaffirmed why this mission matters.

When road safety becomes part of early learning, it becomes a habit.
